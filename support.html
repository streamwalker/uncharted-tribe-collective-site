The app

A release ended before I could order

That is the app working, not the app breaking. Limited releases run for a fixed window and then close. When the countdown reaches zero the collection stops accepting orders and moves to Past Runs, where it stays as a record. We do not reopen a closed release, and we do not reprint one.

I want to know before the next one opens

Turn on release reminders in the app, or add the widget to your Home Screen. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we are not sending anything to a server to make them fire.

Checkout, Apple Pay or Shop Pay isn’t working

Checkout runs on Shopify. If a payment fails, first confirm the card in your Wallet is current and that the shipping address is one we ship to. If it still fails, write to us with the approximate time you tried — that is usually enough to find the attempt on our side.

Where is my order?

Order status, tracking and receipts live in your store account rather than in the app, because that is where Shopify keeps them. Sign in to your account to see them, or send us your order number and we will track it down.

Something is broken

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. Screenshots help more.
